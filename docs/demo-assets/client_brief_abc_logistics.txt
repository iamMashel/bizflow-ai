Client Brief: ABC Logistics AI Assistant

Company:
ABC Logistics is a regional logistics provider that coordinates deliveries for small and midsize retailers. The team manages inbound shipment requests, delivery status updates, customer questions, and internal handoffs across email, spreadsheets, and a lightweight CRM.

Current Problem:
ABC Logistics receives a high volume of repetitive customer questions about shipment status, delivery windows, missed deliveries, invoice follow-ups, and required documentation. Support staff spend too much time searching across emails and spreadsheets before responding. Managers also lack a reliable way to turn customer requests into follow-up actions.

Business Goal:
ABC Logistics wants an AI-assisted workflow that can help the operations team understand client requests faster, draft accurate follow-up responses, and prepare structured handoff notes for internal review.

Desired Solution:
The client is interested in a document-grounded AI assistant that can:
- Answer questions from uploaded client briefs and operational documents.
- Summarize customer requirements and missing information.
- Draft proposal or follow-up emails for review.
- Prepare workflow payloads that can later be approved and sent into automation tools.
- Log approved workflow activity for operational tracking.

Important Requirements:
- The assistant must only answer from approved uploaded documents.
- The system must not send emails without human approval.
- External automations must be reviewable before execution.
- The first workflow should log approved activity to Google Sheets.
- The team prefers a phased rollout before connecting CRM or Gmail actions.

Missing Information:
- Preferred implementation timeline.
- Exact CRM used by the operations team.
- Whether Google Sheets should be the long-term reporting destination.
- Who should approve outgoing workflow actions.
- Which customer request categories should be prioritized first.

Recommended Next Steps:
- Confirm the CRM and reporting tools.
- Define the first three customer request categories.
- Approve a pilot workflow that logs reviewed AI outputs to Google Sheets.
- Review generated email drafts before connecting Gmail automation.
